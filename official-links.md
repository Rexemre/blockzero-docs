## Warning: copycat sites & unofficial services

**Copycat websites** exist that mimic Block Zero — often with look-alike names and different domain endings such as **`.cc`**, **`.io`**, **`.net`**, or similar. These sites are **not** operated by Block Zero and have **no affiliation** with this project.

Any miners, pools, wallets, explorers, or downloads offered on such sites:

- Have **nothing to do with us**
- Are **not** reviewed, audited, or endorsed by us — we have **no insight into their code**
- May contain **malware**, steal keys or wallet data, or connect you to a **fake or wrong blockchain**

### We accept no liability

Use of unofficial sites or services is entirely at your own risk. Block Zero assumes **no responsibility** for harm arising from them, including but not limited to:

- **Viruses, trojans, and other malware**
- **Mining on the wrong chain** or a scam network that is not Block Zero mainnet
- **Fraud and scams** of any kind (phishing, fake support, counterfeit binaries)
- **Third-party mining pools** — operators may dilute or withhold your fair share, manipulate payout logic, or enrich themselves at miners' expense; such behaviour is often **impossible for you to verify**

### How to stay safe

1. **Only trust URLs on this page** and on [bloz.org](https://bloz.org).
2. Official web services use the **`bloz.org`** domain only (e.g. `pool.bloz.org`, `explorer.bloz.org`, `bridge.bloz.org`).
3. Download binaries only from [blockzero-core Releases](https://github.com/Rexemre/blockzero-core/releases) or the official [blockzero-ops](https://github.com/Rexemre/blockzero-ops) scripts.
4. Prefer **solo mining** or the **official pool** via our GitHub scripts — not unknown third-party pools.
5. When in doubt, ask in the official [Discord](https://discord.gg/FbJzrwAU2W) before running software or entering a payout address.
